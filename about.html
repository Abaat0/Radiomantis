the mayor won't listen

Mantises are a family of over 2400 species, distributed worldwide. We may not have wings, but all mantodeans have forelegs that are greatly enlarged and adapted for playing music.

Radio Mantis was founded in 2025 in a cozy student dorm in Aarhus, Denmark, and has since grown to host regular shows from residents based everywhere from Los Angeles, Brooklyn, São Paulo, Hamburg, Bangalore and more.

Radio Mantis is now based in Turin, Italy, but stays faithful to building a global community of enthusiastic DJs and selectors who want to share their own musical vision.
