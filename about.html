Mantises are a family of over 2400 species, distributed worldwide. We may not have wings, but all mantises have forelegs that are greatly enlarged and adapted for playing music.


Radio Mantis was founded in 2025 in a cozy student dorm in Aarhus, Denmark and have since then expanded to a global reach hosting regular shows from residents based everywhere from Los Angeles, Brooklyn, São Paulo, Hamburg, Bangalore and more. Radio Mantis is now based in Turin, Italy, but stands faithful to creating a global community of enthusiastic djs and selectors wanting to share their unique musical vision.

We are always looking for fresh talent, so if you are a producer, dj, selector or if you just want to have a chat with us, please send us a message at play@radiomantis.com!
